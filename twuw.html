Getting to MITEC

Please refer to the transportation options below when planning your journey to MITEC. This page is designed for casual labour staff, so the route information is kept direct and useful, instead of making people dig through pretty posters and vague venue wording.

Available Transportation Options

Bus

Ride bus 821 or 851 and stop at KL 1022 MATRADE South bus stop. From there, MITEC is approximately a 4-minute walk.

MRT

Stop at Semantan Station and continue your journey to MITEC by taxi or e-hailing service.

KTM

Stop at Segambut Station and transfer to MITEC using a taxi or e-hailing vehicle.

Taxi / E-Hailing

Staff may request drop-off at the North Entrance or South Entrance of MITEC from anywhere in Kuala Lumpur or Petaling Jaya.

Car

MITEC is accessible through major highways including DUKE, NKVE, SPRINT Highway, and Jalan Kuching.

Travel Tip

On event days, staff should leave earlier than they think is necessary. Around MITEC, “I still have time” is often the exact sentence said before being trapped in traffic. For public transport users, MRT or KTM plus Grab is usually the cleaner move.
