Behind the World-Class Stage

Setting the Benchmark for World-Class Event Crew Values

Spanning 52,000 m² of exhibition and convention space across 11 expansive halls, the Malaysia International Trade and Exhibition Centre (MITEC) is the flagship development of KL Metropolis. Designed with architectural inspiration drawn from the rubber seed and traditional songket weave, MITEC stands as an iconic landmark for international commerce.

As a key economic catalyst for Malaysia's business tourism, MITEC hosts hundreds of international and regional events annually, welcoming millions of delegates, trade buyers, and visitors to Kuala Lumpur.

By bringing your energy, dedication, and talent to our show floors, you become the essential force behind our shared success. Connected by our core values and driven by cutting-edge workforce innovations, together we cultivate a culture of operational precision while striving every day to uphold and continuously elevate MITEC’s world-class reputation.
