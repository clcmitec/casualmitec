MITEC Student Ambassador Programme

Step onto the stage of Malaysia’s premier trade and event hub. Gain hands-on operational experience, enjoy flexible work shifts with dedicated campus transport, and earn performance rewards that boost your financial independence while quietly broadening your professional horizons.

Apply Now

Why Join the Programme?

Designed specifically to fit around university course schedules while providing premium perks and financial rewards.

Competitive & Fast Pay

Earn premium hourly rates with reliable digital shift tracking and swift payout schedules directly tied to your portal profile.

Direct Campus Shuttles

Dedicated direct transportation service provided to and from partner colleges and campus pick-up points across Klang Valley.

Project Bonuses & Incentives

Student Ambassadors are entitled to exclusive performance bonuses and financial incentives for every event project completed.

100% Flexible Shifts

Choose your shifts via our digital booking system. Work on weekends or during semester breaks without affecting your classes.

Certified Experience

Receive official certificates of accomplishment and reference letters for top-performing student crew members upon graduation.

Eligibility Criteria

We are searching for enthusiastic, reliable student leaders currently enrolled in institutions across Kuala Lumpur and Selangor.

Active Student

Currently enrolled in a Diploma or Degree programme at an accredited university or college (preference given to Klang Valley campuses).

Malaysian Citizen / PR

Must be aged 18 and above with valid NRIC and an active personal Malaysian bank account for automated payouts.

Communication

Conversational fluency in English and Bahasa Malaysia. Fluency in additional languages is a strong added advantage.

Positive Mindset

Punctual, energetic, customer-centric, and eager to thrive in high-profile, fast-paced international event environments.

What You'll Be Doing

Gain hands-on experience across diverse operational areas while supporting exhibitions, conventions, corporate functions and international events at MITEC.

Delegate Registration

Welcome delegates and VIPs, support digital check-in and assist with event registration.

Guest Experience & Wayfinding

Welcome visitors, provide directions and help guests navigate MITEC’s exhibition halls and event spaces.

Banquet & Event Operations

Support gala dinners, conferences, corporate events and VIP functions while gaining practical event-service experience.

Culinary Experience

Work alongside MITEC’s culinary team and gain practical exposure to food preparation, kitchen operations and large-scale event catering.

Back-of-House Operations

Be part of what happens behind the scenes, supporting event set-up, service preparation, logistics, equipment movement and operational coordination.

Event & Hall Support

Support guest movement, hall access and event operations while helping create a safe, organised and welcoming experience.

Convenient Campus Access

MITEC is strategically located in KL Metropolis, easily accessible via major highways and direct public transit links from major Klang Valley universities (UM, TAR UMT, Taylor's, Sunway, UiTM, KDU, and APU).

Direct Campus Shuttle Service

Direct transportation service is provided to and from designated college partner hubs, ensuring hassle-free travel for all active student crew members.

Fast-Track Student Onboarding

Register online through this portal, submit your student ID, complete a 15-minute digital briefing, and begin booking your preferred event shifts immediately.

Empowering the Next Generation
