Eligibility Criteria

We are searching for enthusiastic, reliable student leaders currently enrolled in institutions across Kuala Lumpur and Selangor.

Currently enrolled in a Diploma or Degree programme at an accredited university or college (preference given to Klang Valley campuses).

Malaysian Citizen / PR
